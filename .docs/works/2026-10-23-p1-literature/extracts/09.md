Says: The paper looks at recommending items to new users who have bought nothing yet. These users give only basic side information, such as age or social network data. The authors write neighborhood-based methods in a matrix framework that does not need purchase data for these users. On real data from 30,000 users, using Facebook page likes gave large gains over simple baselines. The gains are reported in the abstract only; the full text was not available to me.

The model is a neighborhood-based method for cold-start collaborative filtering, written in a generalized matrix algebra framework. Source: Abstract (no section or page number; abstract only).
