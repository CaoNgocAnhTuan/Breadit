Says:
The hot function scores a post from its net votes (ups minus downs). The log10 of the larger of that net score and 1 gives the size of the vote part, and its sign (+1, -1 or 0) is applied to it. A time term is added: the post's epoch seconds minus 1134028003, divided by 45000. Because the time term grows with the date, newer posts get a higher score for the same votes.
